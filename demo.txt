**Career Growth Navigator** is an innovative software system designed to automate and personalize employee career development within organizations. By aggregating and analyzing multi-source feedback—such as self-assessments, peer reviews, and manager evaluations—using advanced natural language processing, the system categorizes employee performance across key dimensions and identifies specific strengths and growth areas. It then generates actionable, individualized career progression roadmaps with clear goals and measurable milestones, which are dynamically updated as new feedback is received. The system seamlessly integrates with existing HR platforms, provides an intuitive user interface for tracking progress, and delivers real-time guidance, ensuring employees receive tailored, data-driven support for their professional growth. This solution addresses the limitations of traditional performance review systems by offering continuous, objective, and highly personalized career advancement recommendations at scale.
