### 4. System Challenges and Limitations of Traditional Approaches

- **Infrequent Feedback**: Reviews are typically annual or semi-annual, missing ongoing changes and improvements.
- **Fragmented Data**: Feedback comes from multiple, disconnected sources, making it hard to see the complete picture.
- **Subjectivity and Bias**: Evaluations rely on individual opinions, leading to inconsistencies and potential unfairness.
- **Manual, Time-Consuming Processes**: Gathering and analyzing feedback by hand increases HR workload and delays insights.
- **Generic Development Plans**: Growth actions are often “one size fits all,” not tailored to individual needs or ambitions.
- **Limited Transparency**: Employees rarely have visibility into their progress or how to advance.
- **Low Employee Engagement**: Lack of involvement in development planning causes disengagement and slows growth.
- **Poor Scalability**: Manual and inconsistent processes do not scale well for large organizations.
