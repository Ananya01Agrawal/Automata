Abstract
The Career Growth Navigator is an AI-driven platform that transforms the traditional approach to employee development within large organizations. By integrating advanced natural language processing and machine learning with enterprise HR systems like JPMC’s Career & Development section, it continuously aggregates and analyzes feedback from multiple sources—including self-reviews, peer input, and managerial assessments. This AI-driven analysis accurately categorizes feedback, identifies each employee’s strengths and skill gaps, and produces dynamic, personalized career roadmaps with specific, actionable steps for growth.

Unlike conventional models, Career Growth Navigator provides real-time updates, allowing both employees and authorized assistants to edit and enhance development plans directly within their official profiles. The system’s automated workflows reduce administrative overhead and bias, improve transparency, and offer clear visualization of progress. Through its seamless integration, the platform ensures career guidance remains current, relevant, and aligned with organizational needs. The result is a scalable, unbiased, and engaging approach to talent development that empowers employees while equipping leadership with timely workforce analytics for strategic planning.
